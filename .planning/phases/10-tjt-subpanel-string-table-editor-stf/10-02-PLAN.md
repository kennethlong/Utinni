<objective>
Ship the **CLI-level SC4 correctness gate** for Phase 10 (CF-02). This plan introduces
`utinni-cli roundtrip-stf` — the analog of Phase 8 `roundtrip-iff` and Phase 9 `roundtrip-tab` — that
loads a `.stf`, optionally applies a single `--edit-text KEY=VALUE` mutation, serializes via 10-01's
`StringTableWriter`, re-parses, and asserts byte-exact identity (whole-file for canonical no-mutate
inputs; per-entry-slice on untouched entries after an edit). The João no-mutate golden is the explicit
SC4 non-ASCII proof; the edit-text golden additionally asserts the D-02b sourceCrc-preserve policy.

Purpose: SC4 ("non-ASCII round-trips cleanly") gets an automated, CI-observable gate that fires on
every push — independent of the WinForms form (which is maintainer-smoke-only). Kept separate from
10-01 per the Phase 9 09-01/09-02 precedent: the CLI gate is the correctness proof and deserves its own
verification surface; it touches a different project so it runs in Wave 1 alongside 10-01.

Output: the verb + Program.cs wire + a CLI-side fixture builder + a golden suite with committed goldens.
</objective>
