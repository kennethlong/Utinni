<objective>
Close R1: on real high-era terrain (naboo.trn, PTAT/0014) the layer-item-header DATA leaf is one
version-form deeper than the Phase-21 fix handles — the on-disk shape is
`LAYR -> <layer-version FORM> -> IHDR -> <IHDR-version FORM> -> DATA`, but both `TgenDecoder.ReadLayerItemHeader`
and `TerrainSaveTargets.ResolveIhdrLeafStableId` assume `DATA` is a DIRECT child of `IHDR`. Result: the
active flag silently reads as the C++ default `true` (editor shows the default, not the real flag) and the
active-flag toggle throws `LAYR FORM '...' has no IHDR DATA child leaf (cannot address active flag)` on save.

Purpose: restore active-flag read + write correctness on real terrain while preserving every shipped
golden (the collapsed `WithLayerHeader` shape and the current `WithRealLayrWrapper` direct-DATA shape).

Output: a new RED-first synthesizer fixture modelling the deeper nesting, a decoder descent + a mirrored
resolver descent (both with a direct-DATA fallback), and the local test-mirror updated to match.
</objective>
